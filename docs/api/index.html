Up to three selections per day, never more than one per match. Strong Over 2.5 goals selections come first (best value first; other goal lines are not used); any places left go to the book’s 1 / X / 2 pick with high or medium confidence, odds between 1.80 and 4.00, a bookmaker margin of 8% or less, and value according to the ratings model (its chance for that result × odds ≥ 1.05), best value first. If nothing qualifies, the list stays short or empty. Every Top 3 selection is tracked under Paper betting.

Market moves and traps

The site keeps the first price it sees for each match and compares it with every later update. Tested on 2022–26 Bet365 prices: when the whole market shortened an Over 2.5 price by 8%+, those matches went over more often than even the final price said, and backing Under against the move lost 9–25%; a bet whose price drifted 5%+ lost 12% at the early price. So picks the market is moving against are dropped from the Top 3, the Over 2.5 daily 3 and the weekend slip, and every pick shows “✓ market moving towards” or “⚠ moving against”. One possible real trap remains: Bet365 shortening the price on its own while the rest of the market does not follow (public money). In the test that happened only 60 times (Under +4%, not proven), so these are listed separately as “possible traps” and tracked on paper. Each pick also shows both teams’ last 8 matches (goals per game, Over 2.5 record, goals conceded) so you can judge the style of the teams yourself.

Weekend slip

On Saturdays and Sundays, an accumulator of 10–12 selections aiming at total odds of 40–60. Each match offers its likeliest selection: Over 2.5 goals or the favourite to win, each with a chance that averages our model and the bookmaker’s, at odds 1.20–1.70 (best price where known). Selections are added from the likeliest down until the total odds reach the target. High risk: every selection must win, so a slip wins only about once in 50–100 weekends, and because the bookmaker’s margin is multiplied across all selections, expect to lose roughly 30–40% of the total staked over time. Tracked under Paper betting at €10 per slip.

Over 2.5 goals — the day’s 3 most likely

Every match day, the three matches most likely to have 3 or more goals, ranked by the average of our goals model and the bookmaker’s own chance (margin removed). This list is always filled when there are at least three matches with odds; it does not require value. Over half of all matches end with 3+ goals, so the aim is to find the likeliest ones. Tested on 2022–24 with real Bet365 prices (593 match days): these picks won 61.6% of the time at average odds of about 1.59, which returned −3.8% at Bet365 and −1.0% at the best price; all three won on about 1 day in 4. Break-even at 1.59 is 63%, so use the best price. Tracked separately under Paper betting.

Value goal lines (not from the book)

The ratings model gives the chance of every scoreline, so it can price every goal line: Over/Under 1.5, 2.5 and 3.5. For each match the site checks Over/Under 1.5, Under 2.5 and Over/Under 3.5 and keeps the one with the best value, if it passes: odds 1.80–3.00, our chance × odds 1.08 or more (stricter than Over 2.5 because picking the best of five lines flatters the winner), bookmaker margin 8% or less, and not a cup match. On the 2024 test season the chances for every line matched the actual rates to within about 1 point. These lines are not used in the Top 3: tested against real historical Bet365 prices, Under 2.5 value picks lost 13–16%, and the 1.5 / 3.5 lines could not be tested. They stay on the site and are tracked under Paper betting, so they can come back if live results prove them.

Data sources and “price beats the market average”

This site runs on free files from football-data.co.uk: this season’s results and upcoming fixtures with Bet365, best and average odds for about 38 leagues, with no API key needed. A second version (link at the top) runs on API-Football, covering 1,000+ competitions including cups, and resumes when that account works again. Team form there uses league matches only, and odds are updated a few times a week, so always check the current price before betting. It also lists matches where the best price on the market is at least 4% above the market-average chance (margin removed): in the 2022–24 test this returned +10.5% over 302 bets, which is promising but not proven, so it is tracked on paper as an experiment.

Price beats Pinnacle

Pinnacle takes big bets from professionals and moves its prices on their money, so its odds with the margin removed are the best available estimate of each result’s true chance. Once a day per league (free plan of The Odds API, 500 credits a month, top leagues first), the site stores Pinnacle’s and Betfair Exchange’s prices for 1 / X / 2 and Over/Under 2.5. A selection is listed when the best price (or Bet365’s) times Pinnacle’s true chance is 1.02 or more, at odds up to 5. In the 2022–24 test with Pinnacle prices this returned +4.0% over 3,708 bets (+4.5% in 2024); the threshold was set before any live data and will not be tuned on live results. Not proven: tracked under Paper betting.

Which price counts. Picks and the main paper record use Bet365’s price only, because that is the account in use; the “best market price” version is kept only as a comparison, since those prices mostly come from bookmakers that are not available. Be aware: in the 2022–26 history, Bet365 beating Pinnacle by 2% was rare (about 120 times a season across 17 leagues) and lost 6.2% over 466 bets, while the best market price beating Pinnacle returned +4.0% over 4,004 bets. The practical use is the minimum odds list: compare it with Bet365, Stoiximan and Novibet by hand.

Pinnacle often offers its main goals line at 2.25, 2.75 or 3 rather than 2.5. In that case the site estimates Pinnacle’s Over/Under 2.5 chance from that line (assuming goals follow the usual Poisson pattern). Those picks are marked “estimated” and tracked separately on paper, because the historical evidence used real 2.5 prices only.

Over 2.5 goals (not from the book)

A statistical model built on the 2022–24 backtest seasons. It combines the ratings model’s Over 2.5 chance (below) with each team's goals scored and conceded over its last 6 matches (all competitions) and the league's average goals over its last 200 matches. A selection counts as strong only if it passes every check:

  • Both teams have 6+ recorded matches and the league has 30+.
  • Our chance of 3+ goals is 60% or more.
  • Both teams together average 3.0+ goals per game (scored + conceded), and 7+ of their last 12 games went Over 2.5.
  • Bet365 Over 2.5 odds are 1.80 or more (and no more than 2.50).
  • Value: chance × odds is at least 1.05.
  • Bookmaker margin on Over/Under 2.5 is 8% or less.
  • Not a cup match (rotation risk); friendlies are always excluded.

On the 2024 test season, matches passing the chance and form checks went Over 2.5 64.5% of the time (break-even at odds 1.80 is 55.6%). The odds checks can only be proven live, so results are tracked separately.

Ratings model (not from the book)

Every team has an attack and a defence rating, and every league a scoring level and a home advantage. Expected goals for each side come from these; after each match the ratings move a little towards what happened (allowing for how strong the opponent was) and drift slightly back towards average, so a few results don't swing them too far. Expected goals are turned into the chance of every scoreline, and from those the chances of 1, X, 2 and Over 2.5. A team needs 6 recorded matches.

2024 test season: the ratings pick was right 48.4% of the time (always home 44.1%, book’s method 28.6%). It is shown next to the book’s pick and tracked separately; the book stays the source of the 1 / X / 2 selections, and the ratings chance is used to check their value and size their paper stakes.

How predictions are made

Each team's form is its most recent results in all competitions (friendlies excluded), using windows of 5, 6, 7 and 8 matches. A team needs at least 7 recorded matches before it is predicted. Every rule below adds weight to home win (1), draw (X) or away win (2); the highest total is the pick.

  • Balance (pp. 46–48): the result that makes the two 6-match forms, plus this match, as equal as possible.
  • Common zero (p. 47): if neither team has drawn in 6, the draw fills the gap.
  • Trailing team (pp. 79–81): the side with fewer positive results (wins + draws) over 7 does not lose; stronger when the 5-match form confirms it and at home. This is the book's main bet, shown as “Underdog”.
  • Unbeaten 7 vs one loss (p. 81): a side unbeaten in 7 facing a side with exactly one loss in 7 tends to lose.
  • Six wins in a row (p. 68): struggles to win again. Six defeats (p. 69): hard to beat by a moderate opponent.
  • Special forms: home side at 1-1-4 doesn't lose to weaker form (p. 67); 4-1-1 gives up points only to equal or better form (p. 68); 1-2-3 is ready for a 4th loss, 2-1-3 is very hard to beat (p. 70).
  • Back home (p. 70): three straight losses, then a home game → bounce back.
  • Golden sequence (pp. 59–60): results that complete a 4-2-1 form over 7 get a small boost.

Bookmaker chances on this site have the margin removed with the “power” method, which takes more of it from outsiders than from favourites; on historical Bet365 prices it matched real results better than an even split.

These rules are an interpretation of the book's text; the book does not give exact weights. Results are judged on the 90-minute score. Predictions are computed only from matches played before kick-off, so the record cannot see the future. This is a research tool, not betting advice.
